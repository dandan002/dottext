# Product scope and experience

## Outcomes and pilot

Help the user answer research questions, discover useful domestic detail, and identify developments worth investigating. Balance major developments with local perspectives. Over a two-week pilot, record question resolution, claim support, useful local detail, major-country coverage, and findings worth further investigation. Never claim a terminal missed a story without a direct coverage comparison.

| Area | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Countries | Chile and Colombia; Brazil is the next candidate, Poland and Turkey later |
| Markets | Equal editorial attention to FX, rates, and sovereign credit as developments warrant |
| Language | English output from Spanish and available English sources |
| Collection | Hourly discovery and updates; attempt one-month backfill and disclose actual coverage |
| Sources | Accessible official documents and local journalism using RSS, public APIs, and permitted website/document retrieval |
| Briefings | On demand and scheduled; default past 24 hours, seven days when requested |
| Length | Approximately five minutes total across selected countries, with deeper links |
| Research | Keyword search, filters, and cited natural-language answers |
| Live retrieval | Separate, explicit user action beyond the archive |
| Access | Login, private workspace, one initial user; support separate future workspaces |
| Delivery | Portal first |
| Timezone | America/New_York with daylight saving support; configurable per user |
| Budget | $50/calendar month target, advance alerts, then applicable allowance stops |

Outside initial scope: social feeds, paid content feeds, full-document English translation, email distribution, trade recommendations, numerical political probabilities, and a general country sentiment score.

## Briefings

Select countries and a 24-hour or seven-day period; generate now or save a schedule. Display generation time, exact evidence cutoff, countries, estimated reading time, and coverage status. Open with a short cross-country overview, continue with country sections, and add upcoming decisions/releases when useful. Use connected prose without repeated mandatory item labels. Weekly coverage synthesizes developments over time rather than concatenating daily summaries.

Expose Local Color, Macro Outlook, and Political Analysis separately. Preserve saved outputs and their as-of evidence snapshots. Show corrections with links to updated versions.

## Research

Offer keyword results and a natural-language question interface. Filter by country, publication date, source, topic, and document type. Keep matching documents accessible alongside cited answers. State insufficient evidence and offer “Search live sources.” Live results show newly retrieved status and distinct publication/collection times. Authorized accessible results enter the normal pipeline and become searchable after processing.

## Follows, feedback, and operations

Follow stories, themes, and people. Feedback includes useful, irrelevant, follow this story, and exclude this source. Preferences influence future selection and background without suppressing major developments or changing facts. All preferences and feedback are workspace-private.

An early coverage panel displays source health, last successful collection, document counts, failures, backfill gaps, and spending. Distinguish “No material developments” from failed or incomplete collection. Display paused work and reasons.
