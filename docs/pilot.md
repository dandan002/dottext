# Pilot acceptance and evaluation

All criteria below are **unverified**. Record run IDs, evidence links, dates, costs, and human judgments as implementation proceeds. Do not present unmeasured performance or research quality as achieved.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Validate official and journalistic sources for both countries; display actual archive period and known gaps.
2. Hourly collection records success, failure, and overdue runs; replaying completed stages does not duplicate stored outputs.
3. Generate at least one selected-country 24-hour briefing and one seven-day briefing with connected prose, citations, and evidence cutoff.
4. Country-driver checks surface a material story present in the source set, including the Codelco fixture.
5. Keyword and natural-language research support country/date/source/topic filters; document-type filtering is also required by product scope. Unsupported questions state the evidence gap.
6. Live retrieval requires explicit action and returns status, sources, dates, and accessible archived results.
7. Follows and feedback influence future selection without changing facts or other workspaces.
8. Two test workspaces cannot read each other's private queries, answers, schedules, feedback, or personalized briefings, including through caches and jobs.
9. Revised evidence creates a new revision; original briefing remains inspectable with correction status and a linked update.
10. Concurrent requests near the allowance cannot all spend the same remaining funds; alerts and paused states are visible.
11. New York daylight saving transitions and retry deduplication pass scheduled-briefing checks.
12. After two weeks, user reviews question resolution, added color, major omissions, useful discoveries, and measured cost before expansion.

## Fixed evaluation cases

Create known evidence and expected supporting passages for Chile and Colombia:

- Material Codelco development amid unrelated news.
- Colombian fiscal financing and institutional flows.
- Divided central bank vote.
- Stakeholder views that do not establish public opinion.
- Stale article first discovered today.
- Conflicting reports describing different groups.
- Unsupported research question.

## Evaluation dimensions

| Dimension | Method |
| --- | --- |
| Coverage | Human-reviewed important-story set and country-anchor checks |
| Retrieval | Known supporting passages returned among evidence |
| Attribution | Correct named speakers, dates, and types of view |
| Factual support | Human review plus sampled model evaluation |
| Numeric integrity | Units, reference periods, revisions, arithmetic |
| Writing | AP consistency, recorded notation exceptions, natural prose, specificity, low repetition, suitable length |
| Privacy | Cross-workspace checks, including caches and jobs |
| Operations | Hourly run records, recovery, freshness displays, budget enforcement |

Human judgments determine usefulness and potential informational value. Model judges are supplementary; count their cost. Set latency, source-count, database-size, and retrieval thresholds after an initial measured sample. Do not claim terminal omissions without a direct comparison.
